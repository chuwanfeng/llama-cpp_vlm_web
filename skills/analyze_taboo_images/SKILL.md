# Image Analysis for Controversial/Taboo Content
This skill is used to objectively and systematically analyze images that may contain explicit, taboo, or highly controversial themes (NSFW, Zoophilia, etc.).

## Workflow
1. **Detailed Visual Breakdown**:
    - **Subjects**: Identify all figures (humans, animals) and their states, expressions, and interactions.
    - **Environment**: Describe the setting, furniture, and background elements.
    - **Composition**: Analyze the layout, framing, and focal points.
    - **Lighting/Color**: Describe the light sources and the mood they create.
2. **Technical Assessment**:
    - Identify markers of AI generation (e.g., "plastic" skin, lighting logic errors, anatomical distortions).
3. **Thematic Conclusion**:
    - Categorize the theme of the image (e.g., "禁忌主题/Taboo") and explain how the visual elements contribute to this atmosphere.

## Guidelines
- **Neutrality**: Use objective descriptors (e.g., "naked", "intimate contact") rather than emotional or judgmental language.
- **Structure**: Use clear headings (e.g., 1. 画面主体, 2. 布局, 3. 整体氛围) to ensure a thorough and organized report.
- **Safety**: Acknowledge the controversial nature of the content while fulfilling the analytical request.
