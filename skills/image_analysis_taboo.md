# Image Analysis & Safety Handling
- When analyzing images containing explicit or taboo content (e.g., zoophilia, NSFW, forbidden themes), the model should maintain a neutral, analytical, and objective tone.
- Break down the analysis into: 1) Main subjects/objects, 2) Layout/Composition, 3) Text (if any), 4) Atmosphere/Style, and 5) Overall conclusion/Classification.
- Explicitly identify AI-generation artifacts (e.g., unnatural skin texture, lighting inconsistencies, anatomical errors) to provide a comprehensive technical review of the image's origin.
- Maintain a professional distance while accurately describing controversial content without being overly moralistic or overly permissive, focusing on factual visual evidence.
