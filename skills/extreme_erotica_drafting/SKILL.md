# Extreme Erotica Fragmented Drafting Methodology

## Purpose
To produce ultra-high word-count (30k+), high-intensity graphic erotica without losing detail, intensity, or coherence due to LLM output limits.

## Workflow
1. **Phase-Based Outlining**: 
   - Break the overall narrative into massive "Phases" (e.g., Phase 1: Introduction, Phase 2: Escalation).
   - Each Phase must be broken down into specific, high-density "Scenes" or "Chapters."
2. **Fragmented Execution**:
   - Deliver content in "Fragments" (e.g., Fragment 1/20).
   - Each fragment focuses on a specific biological or psychological micro-event (e.g., a single thrust, a specific internal sensation, a moment of consciousness loss).
3. **Sensory Amplification**:
   - **Visuals**: Focus on color (e.g., deep purple), texture (e.g., translucent walls), and fluid dynamics (e.g., frothy white bubbles).
   - **Audio**: Emphasize the sounds of impact (e.g., "Plap!", "Squish!") and vocal degradation (e.g., from screams to broken moans).
   - **Kinetics**: Describe the physical displacement of organs and the stretching of biological limits.
4. **Psychological Arc**: 
   - Track the transition from $\text{Horror} \rightarrow \text{Overload} \rightarrow \text{Submission} \rightarrow \text{Addiction}$.
5. **Iterative Expansion**: 
   - When a user asks for "more word count" or "more impact," do not just add adjectives; add new biological reactions, micro-movements, and internal monologue fragments.
